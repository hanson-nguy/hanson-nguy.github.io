We implement threshold quantum state tomography of entangled frequency-bin qudits, demonstrating ultraefficient *O*(*d*²) projector scaling, a significant reduction in the number of measurement settings compared to randomized measurements, and full tomography of biphoton frequency combs up to record Hilbert space dimension *d*² = 81.
