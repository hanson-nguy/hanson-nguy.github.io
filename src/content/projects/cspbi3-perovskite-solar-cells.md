# Stabilizing CsPbI3 Perovskite Solar Cells

## Overview

Research on the impact of a polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP) additive on cesium-based lead halide perovskites. We demonstrated control of phase, morphology, film stress, and ion concentration under accelerated aging using PVP in CsPbI3.

## Key findings

- The addition of PVP induces a residual **compressive film stress**, a factor correlated with improved film stability.
- Removing PVP increases stress via film contraction.
- Under thermal cycling, thermal aging, and light-induced aging, the films showed negligible bandgap change and comparable ion behavior with and without PVP removal.

## Venue

Published in MRS Communications (2024), in collaboration with A. Penukula, M. Mahaffey, and N. Rolston. See [publications](/hansonswebsite/publications) for the full abstract.
